## What it changes

- **Interface:** every panel, frame, button and tab in near-pure black, with gold as the one accent colour.
- **Login screen:** a lone samurai in front of a gold full moon.
- **Minimap orbs:** each orb is a small gold moon with a black pictogram. Hitpoints, prayer and special attack have their own outline colour, and status effects such as poison keep their warning colour.
- **Compass:** shows N, E, S and W in gold.
- **Friends, ignore and clan icons:** hand-drawn samurai silhouettes in front of the moon.
- **Inventory:** the moon scene, dimmed, sits behind the side panel.
- **Skill, settings and equipment icons:** brightened and outlined in gold so they stay readable on black.
- **Attack styles:** the selected style keeps its black box and is marked with a gold lining.

## Notes

- The chatbox is kept a warm grey rather than black, because the game draws NPC dialogue in black text.
- Made for the resizable layouts; the classic fixed layout is recoloured too.
